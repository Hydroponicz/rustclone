Survival tips

  • Gunshots carry far. Every zombie in earshot will come looking.
  • Crouching keeps you quiet and harder to spot. Your flashlight gives you away at night.
  • Raise your foundations: zombies can't climb, but they will bash through twig walls.
  • Headshots drop the dead. Leg shots leave them crawling.
  • Bites can infect you. There is no cure.
